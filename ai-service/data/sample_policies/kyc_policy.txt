
SAMPLE BANKING POLICY
Policy ID: KYC-001
Version: 1.0

Title: Customer KYC Verification Policy

1. Purpose
This sample policy describes basic customer identity verification
requirements for a fictional bank.

2. Required Documents
A customer must provide an approved identity document and the
additional documents required by the bank's verification process.

3. Verification
The bank must verify the submitted information before approving
the customer's account application.

4. Failed Verification
If verification fails, the application must remain pending until
the discrepancy is resolved by an authorized employee.

5. Human Review
Cases involving inconsistent identity information must be referred
to an authorized reviewer.

IMPORTANT:
This is a fictional policy created for software testing.
It is not an official banking or regulatory policy.
