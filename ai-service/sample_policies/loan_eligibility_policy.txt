1. CORE UNDERWRITING PHILOSOPHY
BankCore360 follows prudent credit risk management guidelines designed to prevent retail customer over-indebtedness while promoting fair access to credit. Indicative eligibility estimates calculated online are preliminary assessments only and never constitute an official credit sanction.

2. MINIMUM INCOME CRITERIA
- Salaried Applicants: Minimum net verifiable monthly take-home income of ₹15,000 for Personal and Education loans; ₹35,000 for Home loans.
- Self-Employed Individuals / Business Owners: Minimum audited net annual taxable income of ₹2,50,000 as declared in verified Income Tax Returns (ITR).

3. DEBT-TO-INCOME AND FOIR RESTRICTIONS
- The primary lending boundary is the Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR):
  FOIR = ((Total Existing Monthly EMIs + Proposed New EMI) / Net Monthly Income) * 100
- Maximum allowable FOIR threshold is strictly 50.0% for monthly incomes up to ₹50,000.
- For applicants with net monthly incomes exceeding ₹50,000, credit managers may permit FOIR up to 55.0% for secured Home Loans.
- Applications with FOIR exceeding 50.0% are automatically flagged by system underwriting as HIGH_OBLIGATION_RISK and will be rejected unless the requested tenure is lengthened or the loan amount reduced.

4. CREDIT SCORE AND DELINQUENCY BENCHMARKS
- Preferred Credit Bureau Score: 700 and above.
- Acceptable Score with Risk Premium: 650 to 699 (attracts 0.50% - 1.00% interest rate mark-up).
- Sub-Prime / High Risk: Below 650. Requires joint solvent guarantor or 100% tangible collateral.
- Delinquency History: Zero instances of 30+ Days Past Due (DPD) in the trailing 12 months. Any settled, written-off, or compromised credit account is grounds for outright rejection.

5. FINAL APPROVAL DISCLOSURE AND GOVERNANCE
Under no circumstances may an AI assistant, marketing portal, or customer service representative promise or guarantee loan sanction. Final credit approval is subject to:
1. Satisfactory document verification by an authorized Checker.
2. Property title search and valuation report (for secured loans).
3. Formal approval and sanction order executed by an authorized Credit Manager.
