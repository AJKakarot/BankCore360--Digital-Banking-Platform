1. MANDATORY POLICY DIRECTIVE
Under BankCore360 dual control principles, every retail or commercial loan application must have all supporting identity and financial documents scrutinized, authenticated, and verified by a designated Checker before any file is forwarded to the Credit Manager for final sanction.

2. ACCEPTABLE IDENTIFICATION PROOFS (OFFICIALLY VALID DOCUMENTS - OVD)
- Primary Identity Proof: Test PAN Card (mandatory for all income-generating borrowers to confirm tax identification).
- Secondary Identity / Address Proof: Test Aadhaar Card (with masked first 8 digits), Voter Identity Card, Driving License, or Valid Passport.
- Utility Bills: Electricity, piped gas, or landline bills issued within the preceding 90 days are acceptable address verifications for residential addresses.
- Strict Prohibition: Uploading real identification numbers or unredacted government credentials in the educational sandbox is strictly prohibited. Synthetic dummy test files must be used.

3. FINANCIAL AND INCOME DOCUMENT STANDARDS
- Salaried Borrowers: Last 3 calendar months computer-generated salary pay-slips displaying company name, employee ID, Gross Pay, Deductions, and Net Pay. Alternatively, latest Form 16 or verified ITR-V acknowledgment.
- Bank Statements: 6 months continuous operative savings or salary account statement in non-editable PDF format. Must clearly show regular monthly salary credit entries matching the submitted payslips.
- Self-Employed Borrowers: Latest 2 years computation of total income, audited P&L and Balance Sheet, accompanied by 6 months bank statements.

4. UPLOAD SPECIFICATIONS AND TECHNICAL RULES
- Supported File Extensions: PDF, JPEG, PNG only. Executable formats (EXE, SH, BAT), scripts, macro-enabled spreadsheets, or encrypted/password-protected PDFs are rejected immediately upon upload.
- File Size Constraints: Minimum 10 KB; Maximum 5 MB per document.
- Image Quality: Must be legible with all edges visible. Document text must not be blurred or cropped.

5. GROUNDS FOR DOCUMENT REJECTION BY CHECKER
A document will be formally rejected by the Checker with a mandatory audit reason if:
1. The applicant name on the document does not match the registered Customer name.
2. The document is expired, illegible, obscured, or cropped.
3. Salary slip does not demonstrate net monthly salary meeting the ₹15,000 threshold.
4. Bank statement demonstrates continuous cheque bounces, negative balance events, or undisclosed existing loans.
5. Suspected digital tampering or mismatched font weights across numeric values.
