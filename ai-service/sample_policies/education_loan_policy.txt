1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The BankCore360 Education Loan supports meritorious students securing admission to recognized undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degree programs in premier domestic and overseas educational institutions.

2. ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
- Student must hold Indian citizenship and have secured confirmed admission through competitive entrance examination or merit selection.
- Co-borrower: Mandatory parent, guardian, or earning spouse as joint co-applicant.
- Minimum Co-borrower Net Monthly Income: ₹25,000.
- Eligible Courses: UGC/AICTE approved degrees, accredited medical, engineering, management, STEM, and international university courses.

3. FINANCING LIMITS AND MORATORIUM
- Domestic Studies Maximum: Up to ₹15,00,000.
- Overseas Studies Maximum: Up to ₹30,00,000 (higher amounts supported against tangible security collateral).
- Margin Requirement:
  * Up to ₹4 Lakhs: Nil margin (100% bank funding).
  * Above ₹4 Lakhs (Domestic): 5% borrower contribution.
  * Above ₹4 Lakhs (Overseas): 15% borrower contribution.
- Moratorium Period (Repayment Holiday): Course duration plus 6 months, or 6 months after securing employment, whichever is earlier.
- Maximum Repayment Tenure: Up to 120 months (10 years) following the moratorium completion.

4. INTEREST RATES AND SUBSIDIES
- Annual Rate of Interest: 9.00% to 11.25% per annum.
- Concession: 0.50% special rate discount for female students.
- Simple interest is charged during the moratorium period.

5. VERIFICATION DOCUMENTATION
- Confirmed Admission Offer Letter and fee structure schedule signed by the registrar.
- Student academic records: 10th, 12th, graduation mark sheets, and entrance exam scorecards.
- Co-applicant KYC: Test PAN, Test Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips or ITR.
- Passport and student visa copy for foreign education applications.
