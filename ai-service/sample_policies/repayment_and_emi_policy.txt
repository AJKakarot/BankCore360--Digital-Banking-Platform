1. EMI CALCULATION FORMULATION
Equal Monthly Installments (EMI) across all BankCore360 retail and commercial loans are computed using the standard mathematical formula:
E = P * r * (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)
Where:
- E = Monthly Equal Installment amount (rounded to nearest Rupee).
- P = Principal Loan Sanction Amount.
- r = Monthly Interest Rate, defined as Annual Interest Rate / 1200.
- n = Total Tenure expressed in months.

2. REPAYMENT SCHEDULE GENERATION
Upon loan disbursement authorization by the Branch Manager:
- The core banking system generates an immutable, complete monthly repayment schedule for the entire tenure.
- Each schedule entry tracks: Installment Number, Due Date (typically the 5th or 10th of each calendar month), Total EMI Due, Principal Component, Interest Component, and Outstanding Closing Principal Balance.
- Installment payments are applied first towards accrued interest and penal fees, with the residue applied to extinguish principal balance.

3. REPAYMENT MODES AND AUTOMATION
- Primary Mode: Electronic Clearing Service (NACH / e-Mandate) auto-debit linked to the customer's registered bank account.
- Alternate Channels: Immediate Payment Service (IMPS), National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT), UPI, or online payment portal.
- Standing Instructions: Customer must ensure adequate balance in their designated repayment account at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled monthly debit date.

4. PART-PREPAYMENT AND FULL CLOSURE
- Borrowers may make partial principal prepayments at any time to either lower future monthly EMIs or compress remaining tenure.
- Floating rate retail loans carry zero foreclosure or prepayment penalty.
- Fixed rate commercial facilities carry a prepayment compensation charge of 2.0% on prepaid principal if closed within 12 months of sanction.

5. DELINQUENCY AND DEFAULT MANAGEMENT
- Day 1 to 30 past due: Grace period with SMS and email reminders.
- Day 31 to 60 past due: Account flagged as Special Mention Account (SMA-1). Formal demand notice issued.
- Day 90 past due: Account classified as Non-Performing Asset (NPA) requiring formal legal recovery proceedings.
