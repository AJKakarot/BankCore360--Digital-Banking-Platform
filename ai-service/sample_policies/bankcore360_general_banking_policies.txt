# BankCore360 Comprehensive Digital Banking Policies & Terms: Section 1 Account Types & Features
1.1 Savings Account:
- Minimum average monthly balance: ₹5,000 for Metro branches, ₹2,500 for Semi-Urban branches.
- Interest Rate: 4.00% p.a. calculated on daily end-of-day balances and credited quarterly.
- Free facilities: Unlimited digital NEFT/RTGS transfers, free monthly electronic bank statements, and 5 free ATM withdrawals per month at BankCore360 ATMs.

1.2 Current Account:
- Tailored for business operations, traders, and corporate enterprises.
- Minimum quarterly average balance: ₹25,000.
- Features: Overdraft facility against approved collateral, bulk payroll upload, integrated corporate internet banking, and daily withdrawal limit of ₹2,00,000.

1.3 Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA):
- Zero minimum balance requirement with no penalty charges.
- Available for all eligible residents under financial inclusion mandates.
- Maximum 4 debit transactions per calendar month.

## Section 2: Service Charges & Fee Matrix
2.1 Maintenance & Minimum Balance Penalty:
- Failure to maintain minimum balance attracts a monthly charge of ₹150 + 18% GST.
- Annual Debit Card maintenance fee: Classic Card ₹250/year, Platinum Card ₹500/year.

2.2 Remittance & Fund Transfer Charges:
- NEFT: Free online 24x7x365 via Mobile Banking and Internet Banking. Branch NEFT charges ₹2.50 to ₹25 based on slab.
- RTGS: Free online 24x7 for transactions exceeding ₹2,00,000.
- IMPS: ₹3.00 for transactions up to ₹1,000; ₹5.00 for ₹1,001 to ₹25,000; ₹15.00 for ₹25,001 to ₹5,00,000 (+ GST).
- International Inward Wire Transfers: Free. Outward Wire Transfers (LRS): Flat ₹500 processing fee + applicable FX margin.

## Section 3: Deposit & Withdrawal Rules
3.1 Cash Deposit Limits:
- Up to ₹50,000 per day cash deposit accepted without mandatory PAN submission.
- Cash deposits between ₹50,001 and ₹2,00,000 require valid Form 60 or PAN card verification.
- Monthly cash deposit limit at base branch: ₹5,00,000 free, beyond which ₹3 per ₹1,000 applies.

3.2 Cash Withdrawal Limits:
- Daily ATM cash withdrawal limit: ₹50,000 for Classic Debit Cards, ₹1,00,000 for Platinum Debit Cards.
- Daily POS / E-Commerce transaction limit: ₹2,00,000.

## Section 4: Transaction Dispute, Refund & Reversal Policies
4.1 Reversal & Compensating Entries:
- BankCore360 enforces an immutable double-entry ledger. Posted ledger entries are never modified or deleted.
- If a transaction is disputed or errant, an authorized reversal generates a compensating ledger transaction that restores accurate account balances.
- Erroneous fund transfers: Customer must report within 24 hours. The bank places a temporary hold on the beneficiary account subject to mutual customer consent or inter-bank recovery guidelines.

4.2 Unauthorized / Fraudulent Transactions & Customer Liability:
- Zero Liability: If the customer notifies the bank within 3 working days of receiving unauthorized transaction alert, zero liability applies to the customer, and credit is restored within 10 working days.
- Limited Liability: If reported within 4 to 7 working days, maximum customer liability is capped at ₹5,000 (Savings account) or ₹10,000 (Current account).
- Beyond 7 days: Customer liability is determined in accordance with Board-approved customer protection policy.
- Dispute filing: Customers can file disputes online via Customer Portal (/customer/transactions) or by calling 24x7 helpline 1800-360-BANK.
