1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The BankCore360 Business Loan offers working capital financing, plant and machinery acquisition, inventory build-up, and commercial operational expansion for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), sole proprietorships, partnerships, and private limited entities.

2. ENTERPRISE ELIGIBILITY NORMS
- Business Vintage: Minimum 3 continuous financial years in the same line of commercial activity.
- Minimum Annual Turnover: ₹25,00,000 audited gross turnover.
- Business Financial Health: Entity must report positive net profit (PAT) and positive tangible net worth for the preceding 2 consecutive years.
- Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): 1.35x.
- Promoter Age: Minimum 24 years; Maximum 65 years.

3. LOAN QUANTUM AND STRUCTURE
- Minimum Loan Limit: ₹1,00,000.
- Maximum Unsecured Business Loan: ₹50,00,000.
- Maximum Secured Commercial Term Facility: ₹2,00,00,000 against industrial or commercial real estate collateral.
- Tenure: 12 months to 84 months (7 years).

4. INTEREST MATRIX AND CHARGES
- Interest Rates: 11.00% to 15.50% per annum depending on business credit risk rating and audited balance sheet strength.
- Processing Fee: 1.50% of the sanctioned credit facility.
- Annual Renewal / Facility Inspection Fee: 0.25% of sanctioned working capital limit.

5. STATUTORY & FINANCIAL DOCUMENTATION
- Business Registration: GST Registration Certificate, Shop & Establishment license, or Udyam MSME Registration.
- Entity Constitutional Documents: Partnership Deed, Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association (MOA/AOA).
- Audited Financial Statements: Certified Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts with all auditor notes for the last 2 financial years.
- Banking History: Last 12 months operative current account bank statements.
- Test PAN of enterprise and all active partners/directors.
